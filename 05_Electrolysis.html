Key Equations

Cathode (−): reduction — cation + e⁻ → product
Anode (+): oxidation — anion → product + e⁻
H₂ at cathode: 2H⁺+2e⁻→H₂
O₂ at anode: 2H₂O→O₂+4H⁺+4e⁻
Cl₂ at anode: 2Cl⁻→Cl₂+2e⁻
Cu at cathode: Cu²⁺+2e⁻→Cu

[3 marks] Molten lead(II) bromide is electrolysed. State the product at each electrode and give the half-equation at the cathode.

Cathode: lead (Pb) [1]; Anode: bromine (Br₂) [1]; Half-equation: Pb²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Pb [1].

[3 marks] Copper(II) sulfate solution is electrolysed using carbon electrodes. Describe and explain what is observed at the cathode.

A reddish-brown/pink solid is deposited [1]; the solid is copper [1]; Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu [1].

[3 marks] Concentrated sodium chloride solution (brine) is electrolysed. State the products at each electrode and give one industrial use of the product at the anode.

Cathode: hydrogen [1]; Anode: chlorine [1]; Use of chlorine: manufacture of PVC / bleach / disinfectant / hydrochloric acid [1].
