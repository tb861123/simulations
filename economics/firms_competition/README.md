# Firms, Costs and Competition

One firm's costs and revenue (it moves its output each day towards MC = MR),
a market where firms enter after profits and leave after losses (from
monopoly to perfect competition), and at A level a repeated price game.

## How to run

Open `firms-competition.html` in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install; works offline.

## What it shows

- **One firm**: AC, AVC, MC, AR and MR with the profit or loss rectangle; TR
  and TC with the profit gap; a price taker or a price maker; profit, revenue
  or sales maximisation; shutdown and break-even prices at A level.
- **Market structures**: perfect competition, monopolistic competition,
  oligopoly or monopoly; sunk entry costs, a legal barrier, market size; every
  firm drawn, coloured by profit; price against the lowest AC; number of firms;
  CR4 and HHI; a typical firm's own diagram.
- **Price game** (A level): a payoff matrix with the Nash equilibrium; two
  strategies (cartel, always low, tit for tat, grim trigger, win-stay
  lose-shift, random) with optional mistakes.
- Eight guided investigations and a Revise tab.

## Batch run

Profit against output (peaks at MR = MC); price against the number of firms
(Cournot theory); where a competitive price settles (£31 against the lowest AC
of £30); entry cost against the number of firms; a pricing-strategy tournament.

## The earlier version

The previous version is kept, unchanged, in `original/` with its worksheets, and
is linked from "The model" tab. Its economies-of-scale view, price
discrimination, cartels and the kinked demand curve are not in this version.

## Limits

Identical firms; no product differentiation beyond a steeper demand; all
numbers illustrative. See "The model" tab.
