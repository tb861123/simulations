# Photosynthesis and Respiration

A single, self-contained HTML file (`photosynthesis-respiration.html`) for
biology. Open it in Chrome or Edge, with no install and no internet.

**Standard** follows Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (4BI1),
section 2: **2(e) Nutrition** in flowering plants (photosynthesis, its word and
balanced symbol equations, limiting factors: light intensity, carbon dioxide
and temperature; leaf structure; the practical on oxygen from a water plant),
**2(f) Respiration** (ATP; aerobic and anaerobic respiration in animals and in
plants/yeast; the practical on carbon dioxide and heat from respiring seeds)
and **2(g) Gas exchange** in flowering plants (diffusion, stomata, net gas
exchange by day and night; the hydrogencarbonate indicator practical).
**Advanced** follows OCR A level Biology A (H420) Module 5: **5.2.1
Photosynthesis** (chloroplasts; photosynthetic pigments and their
chromatography with Rf values, PAG6; the light-dependent stage: photosystems,
photolysis, photophosphorylation, ATP and reduced NADP; the Calvin cycle:
rubisco, RuBP, GP, TP; limiting factors and their effect on GP, RuBP and TP)
and **5.2.2 Respiration** (glycolysis, the link reaction, the Krebs cycle,
oxidative phosphorylation and chemiosmosis, anaerobic respiration,
respiratory substrates and RQ). The compensation point is tagged H420: it is
named in the H420 mathematical requirements (Appendix 5d, M3.4), though not in
4BI1. Respirometer work is tagged "A level
practical skills" without a PAG number.

## What it shows

- **🌿 Pondweed bubbles**: pondweed in a boiling tube with sodium
  hydrogencarbonate in a water bath, a lamp on a metre rule, bubbles rising
  from the cut stem and counted for one minute (three counts per setting, a
  results table). LED lamp or a filament bulb that warms the water; room
  lights on or off. Graphs: your counts against distance; against relative
  light intensity (1/d²), with the straight line light alone would give; and
  limiting-factor curves (rate against light at three CO₂ levels and a lower
  temperature). A level shows net O₂ with respiration and the compensation
  point.
- **🍃 Leaf and gas exchange**: a leaf cross-section (cuticle, epidermis,
  palisade with chloroplasts, spongy mesophyll and air spaces, vein, stomata
  and guard cells, labels on/off), with CO₂ and O₂ molecules diffusing through
  the stomata in the direction the net exchange goes. Petroleum jelly on
  either surface. Four hydrogencarbonate indicator tubes (no leaf, dark,
  gauze, full light) change colour over hours. Graphs: CO₂ in each tube over
  time; net gas exchange against light at three temperatures (compensation
  point marked); net exchange through a sunny day.
- **🫁 Respirometer**: organisms on a gauze platform above soda lime (A level:
  or water), a control tube of glass beads, two capillaries with coloured
  drops read every minute for 10 minutes. Germinating peas, germinating
  sunflower seeds, maggots, boiled peas, and yeast without air. Volume =
  πr² × distance; a vacuum-flask tile for the heat released. Graphs: the drop
  against time (organism tube, control, corrected, best-fit line); your rates
  against temperature; (A level) RQ from a soda-lime and a water run, against
  the RQ of carbohydrate, protein and lipid.
- **⚙️ Inside the cell**: a chloroplast and a mitochondrion with moving
  particles. Standard: CO₂ and water in, oxygen and glucose out; glucose to
  ATP with and without oxygen (lactic acid in muscle; ethanol + CO₂ in
  yeast). A level: thylakoid membrane with PSII, PSI and ATP synthase,
  photolysis, electrons and H⁺; the Calvin cycle with live RuBP, GP and TP
  pools; glycolysis, link reaction, Krebs cycle and the electron transport
  chain. Buttons switch the light off or cut the CO₂. Graphs: ATP per glucose
  (stage by stage at A level); RuBP, GP and TP against time; a thin-layer
  chromatogram of leaf pigments (PAG6) with measured distances and Rf values.

Right panel: what's happening, the word and symbol equations, readings,
results tables, key ideas tagged by spec, exam practice (9 GCSE and 8 A level
questions with mark schemes; on-screen numbers for the inverse square law,
indicator colours, respirometer volume, Rf, RQ, Q10 and the compensation
point) and "How the model works (and what it leaves out)". Links to Carbon
Cycle, Chromatography, Enzymes, Membranes and Movement, Gas Exchange, Heart
and Circulation and Central Limit Theorem.

## The models

- Light intensity = 100 × (10/d)² (relative; about 20 000 lux at 10 cm,
  approximate). Gross O₂ = smooth minimum of a light-limited rate (0.6 × I,
  little affected by temperature) and a CO₂/enzyme-limited rate
  55 × f(T) × (C + 0.02)/(C + 0.32), f(T) with Q10 = 2 and denaturing above
  about 38 °C. Bubbles per minute = gross − respiration (3 at 25 °C), counted
  as a Poisson process; pondweed pieces ±10%; a filament bulb warms the water
  by up to 8 °C.
- Leaf: J = 0.00093 × lux; rubisco-limited rate 25 f(T)(Ca − 45)/(Ca + 355);
  respiration 1.0 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ at 20 °C (Q10 = 2); stomata 90% on the lower
  surface. Light compensation point about 1100 lux at 20 °C. Indicator CO₂
  changes by 0.03 ppm s⁻¹ per µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ of net exchange (buffer lumped in).
- Respirometer: O₂ per gram per minute at 20 °C: peas 0.8, sunflower 0.75,
  maggots 1.6 mm³; Q10 = 2; damage above 40–44 °C. RQ: peas 1.0, sunflower 0.7,
  maggots 0.82. Water bath drift ±0.03 °C per minute (Charles' law on 20 cm³
  of air; the control cancels about four-fifths), scale ±0.5 mm, seeds ±8%.
- Calvin cycle: v1 = 0.5 cf R, v2 = 0.8 J G, v3 = 0.4 J P, v4 = 0.4 P²;
  dR/dt = v3 − v1, dG/dt = 2v1 − v2, dP/dt = v2 − v3 − v4.
- ATP: 2 + 2 ATP directly, 10 reduced NAD × 2.5 and 2 reduced FAD × 1.5 →
  about 32 per glucose (38 theoretical); anaerobic 2.
- Chromatography Rf: β-carotene 0.95, xanthophyll 0.71, chlorophyll a 0.53,
  chlorophyll b 0.42 (typical TLC values, approximate); ±2.5% per run.

## Data and sources (approximate)

- Atmospheric CO₂ about 420 ppm (NOAA global monthly mean, 2023).
- RQ values 1.0 / about 0.9 / about 0.7 for carbohydrate / protein / lipid
  (textbook values). ATP yields as in A level textbooks (30–32 real, 38
  theoretical).
- Rf values: typical for thin-layer chromatography of leaf pigments with a
  non-polar solvent; an OCR H420 mark scheme (Nov 2020) uses about 0.52–0.53
  for chlorophyll a. They vary with the solvent.
- Respiration rates, light conversions and photosynthesis constants are
  illustrative, chosen to give realistic proportions.

## Batch run

**📊 Batch run** opens the batch panel (every setting in an editable table;
tick one to sweep; Repeat or Sweep; CSV download). One-click experiments:

1. **The inverse square law:** lamp 5 → 60 cm, 3 counts each: 39 (5 cm),
   37.7 (10 cm), 11.3 (20 cm), 3.7 (30 cm), 0 beyond 45 cm, against the model
   39.0 / 36.9 / 11.8 / 3.6 / 0. Close to the lamp the counts level off well
   below the light-alone line (57 at 10 cm): CO₂ limits.
2. **Carbon dioxide as a limiting factor:** NaHCO₃ 0 → 2% at 10 cm:
   0.5 → 18 (0.2%) → 39 (1%) → 41.5 (2%), theory 0.4 → 19.7 → 36.9 → 41.0.
3. **Temperature and rate:** 5 → 45 °C: 10 → 45 (30–35 °C) → 7, theory
   9.8 → 45.7 → 6.4.
4. **How much do counts scatter?** 200 counts at 20 cm: mean 11.69 (theory
   11.75), SD 3.69 (predicted 3.62), standard error 0.26.
5. **The compensation point:** 0 → 4000 lux at 20 °C: −1.01 → +2.50 µmol
   m⁻² s⁻¹ against −1.00 → +2.57, crossing zero just above 1000 lux.
6. **Respirometer: temperature and Q10:** 5 → 45 °C, means within about 2%
   of the model up to 40 °C (0.28 → 2.76 mm³ g⁻¹ min⁻¹), Q10 ≈ 2, falling at
   45 °C.
7. **RQ of sunflower seeds:** 100 pairs: mean 0.701 (theory 0.70), SD 0.023.
