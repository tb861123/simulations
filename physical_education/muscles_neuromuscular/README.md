# Muscles and the Neuromuscular System

A single, self-contained HTML file: motor units and the all-or-none law, wave summation and tetanus, recruitment by size, slow and fast fibres and fatigue, and the muscle spindles and Golgi tendon organs behind PNF stretching. It needs no install and no
internet: **open `muscles-neuromuscular.html` in Chrome or Edge**. Printable worksheets (one per
investigation and per batch experiment, with teacher notes) are in
`worksheets.html`.

Kit-built: made from `tools/kit/src/muscles_neuromuscular.js` by `node build.js` (run inside
`tools/kit/`). Do not edit `muscles-neuromuscular.html` by hand.

- **Standard (GCSE):** OCR GCSE (9–1) Physical Education (J587) 1.1: the muscular system, types of contraction, slow and fast twitch fibres.
- **Advanced (A level):** OCR A level Physical Education (H555) 1.1: fibre types, motor units, the all-or-none law, spatial and wave summation, tetanus, proprioceptors and PNF.

## What it shows

**One motor unit:** impulses run down a motor neurone and its fibres twitch; turn up the frequency to see wave summation and tetanus for type I, IIa and IIx units, with the force–frequency curve. **Whole muscle:** 120 motor units recruited smallest first to hold a target force; watch them tire by fibre type and see when the target can no longer be held (sprinter's or marathon runner's muscle). **Stretching** (A level): static, PNF and ballistic stretches with muscle spindle and Golgi tendon organ activity, and range gained over six weeks.
