# Nutrition and Ergogenic Aids

A single, self-contained HTML file: a marathon with glycogen and sweat, an 800 m and repeated sprints with legal and banned aids, and why drug tests give false positives. It needs no install and no
internet: **open `nutrition-ergogenic-aids.html` in Chrome or Edge**. Printable worksheets (one per
investigation and per batch experiment, with teacher notes) are in
`worksheets.html`.

Kit-built: made from `tools/kit/src/nutrition_ergogenic_aids.js` by `node build.js` (run inside
`tools/kit/`). Do not edit `nutrition-ergogenic-aids.html` by hand.

- **Standard (GCSE):** OCR GCSE (9–1) Physical Education (J587) 2.2–2.3: a balanced diet, carbohydrate loading, hydration and performance-enhancing drugs.
- **Advanced (A level):** OCR A level Physical Education (H555) 1.2 and 3.2: diet and nutrition, glycogen loading, hydration, ergogenic aids (creatine, caffeine, bicarbonate, nitrate, altitude training, EPO and blood doping) with their risks and legality, and drug testing.

## What it shows

**Race:** a 65 kg runner's marathon minute by minute with glycogen and body-water tanks, hitting the wall, carbohydrate gels, drinking, temperature and seven aids (with type, effect, risks and legality); an 800 m; and ten 30 m sprints limited by phosphocreatine. **Drug testing** (A level): 1000 athletes, the share doping, the test's sensitivity and specificity, true and false positives and Bayes' theorem.

## Batch run

| Experiment | Result when tested (theory) |
|---|---|
| Carbohydrate during the marathon | 3 h 25 min with none, 3 h 10 min with 60 g an hour |
| Dehydration and pace | no loss up to 1.5% of body mass, then 2% slower per extra 1% |
| Creatine and repeated sprints | up to 1.1% less slowing with creatine |
| How trustworthy is a positive test? | 44% of positives real at 0.5% doping, 81% at 2%, 97% at 20% |
| 200 marathon runners, no fuel plan | mean 3 h 24 min, SD 4.5 min |
