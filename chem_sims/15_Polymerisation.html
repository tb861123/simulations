Exam Practice

[3 marks] Describe how addition polymerisation forms poly(ethene) from ethene.

Many ethene monomers (which contain C=C double bonds) join together [1]; the double bonds open up / break to form new single bonds between the molecules [1]; forming one long-chain molecule (polymer) with no other product [1].

[2 marks] State what is meant by the terms monomer and polymer.

A monomer is a small molecule that can join to others [1]; a polymer is a large/long-chain molecule made from many monomers joined together [1].

[2 marks] Poly(ethene)'s repeat unit is –(CH₂–CH₂)–. Explain why addition polymers are difficult to dispose of.

They are unreactive / non-biodegradable [1]; so they are not broken down by microorganisms and persist in the environment (landfill) [1].
