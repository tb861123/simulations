Exam Practice

[3 marks] Explain, in terms of structure and bonding, why metals conduct electricity.

Metals have delocalised (free) electrons [1]; these electrons are free to move through the structure [1]; the moving electrons carry the electrical charge [1].

[3 marks] Explain why metals can be bent and shaped (are malleable).

Metals are made of layers of ions/atoms [1]; the layers can slide over each other when a force is applied [1]; the metallic bond (attraction to the delocalised electrons) is not broken, so the metal stays intact [1].

[2 marks] Magnesium has a higher melting point than sodium. Explain why.

Mg²⁺ ions have a higher charge and release more delocalised electrons than Na⁺ [1]; so the metallic bonding is stronger and more energy is needed to overcome it [1].
