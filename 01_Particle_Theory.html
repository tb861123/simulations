Higher T → faster particles
Particles colour: blue (slow) → red (fast)

[2 marks] Describe the arrangement and movement of particles in a liquid.

Particles are close together but not in fixed positions [1];
they move randomly past each other slowly [1].

[2 marks] Explain why a gas exerts pressure on the walls of its container.

Particles move randomly and collide with the walls [1];
each collision exerts a force, and many collisions per second give a net pressure [1].

[3 marks] Ethanol (b.p. 78°C) and water vapour are both gases at the same temperature. Explain why ethanol vapour diffuses more slowly than water vapour.

At the same temperature, both gases have the same average kinetic energy [1];
ethanol molecules have a greater molar mass [1];
so ethanol molecules move more slowly and diffuse less quickly [1].
