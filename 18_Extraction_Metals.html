Key Equations

C + O₂ → CO₂ (coke burns, heat)
CO₂ + C → 2CO
Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂
CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃ (slag)
More reactive than C → electrolysis; less reactive → reduce with C
Exam Practice

[3 marks] Aluminium is extracted by electrolysis, but iron is extracted by reduction with carbon. Explain why.

Aluminium is more reactive than carbon [1]; so carbon cannot reduce/displace it from its ore [1]; iron is less reactive than carbon, so carbon can reduce iron oxide (a cheaper method) [1].

[3 marks] Name the three solid raw materials added to a blast furnace and the substance that acts as the reducing agent.

Iron ore (haematite, Fe₂O₃), coke (carbon) and limestone (calcium carbonate) [1 for all three]; hot air is blasted in [1]; the reducing agent is carbon monoxide, CO [1].

[2 marks] Explain the role of limestone in the blast furnace.

Limestone decomposes (CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂) and the CaO reacts with acidic impurities such as silicon dioxide [1]; forming molten slag (calcium silicate) which is removed / floats on the iron [1].
