[3 marks] Explain why sodium chloride has a high melting point.

NaCl forms a giant ionic lattice of Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions [1];
there are strong electrostatic attractions between the oppositely charged ions [1];
a large amount of energy is needed to overcome these attractions [1].

[3 marks] Solid NaCl does not conduct electricity, but molten NaCl does. Explain why.

In the solid, ions are fixed in the lattice and cannot move [1];
in the molten state, ions are free to move [1];
moving ions carry charge and so conduct electricity [1].

[2 marks] MgO has a higher melting point than NaCl. Explain why.

Mg²⁺ and O²⁻ ions have higher charges than Na⁺ and Cl⁻ [1];
the electrostatic attraction is stronger, so more energy is needed to break the lattice [1].
