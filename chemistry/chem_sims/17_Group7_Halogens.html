Exam Practice

[2 marks] Describe the colour and physical state of chlorine and bromine at room temperature.

Chlorine is a green (yellow-green) gas [1]; bromine is a red-brown / orange liquid [1].

[3 marks] Chlorine is bubbled through potassium bromide solution. State what is observed, write the ionic equation, and explain in terms of reactivity.

The colourless solution turns orange/yellow as bromine is formed [1]; Cl₂ + 2Br⁻ → 2Cl⁻ + Br₂ [1]; chlorine is more reactive than bromine so it displaces bromine from the solution [1].

[3 marks] Explain why reactivity decreases down Group 7.

Going down the group, atoms have more shells, so the outer shell is further from the nucleus [1]; there is more shielding, so an incoming electron is attracted less strongly [1]; so it is harder to gain an electron, making the halogen less reactive [1].
