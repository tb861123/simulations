Key Facts

2M + 2H₂O → 2MOH + H₂
Reaction produces hydrogen gas + a metal hydroxide (alkaline)
Reactivity INCREASES down Group 1
Bigger atom → outer electron further from nucleus → lost more easily
Exam Practice

[3 marks] Describe what you would observe when a small piece of sodium is added to water containing universal indicator.

The sodium floats and moves around on the surface, fizzing / effervescing [1]; it melts into a ball (heat released) and gradually disappears [1]; the indicator turns purple, showing an alkaline solution (sodium hydroxide) is formed [1].

[3 marks] Explain why reactivity increases going down Group 1.

Going down the group, atoms have more shells so the outer electron is further from the nucleus [1]; there is more shielding, so the outer electron is less strongly attracted / held [1]; so the outer electron is lost more easily, making the metal more reactive [1].

[2 marks] Write a balanced symbol equation for the reaction of potassium with water.

2K + 2H₂O → 2KOH + H₂ [1 for correct formulae, 1 for balancing].
