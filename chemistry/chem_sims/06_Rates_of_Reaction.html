Exam Practice

[4 marks] State two ways to increase the rate of the reaction between marble chips and hydrochloric acid, and explain each using collision theory.

Increase temperature: particles have more kinetic energy [1]; more particles have energy ≥ Ea so more collisions are successful [1].
Increase concentration (or powder the marble for surface area): more particles per unit volume [1]; higher collision frequency [1].

[3 marks] Use the Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution to explain why a small increase in temperature causes a large increase in rate of reaction.

The Maxwell–Boltzmann curve is asymmetric with a long tail [1]; a small increase in temperature shifts the distribution to the right [1]; the number of particles with energy ≥ Ea increases greatly, so many more collisions are successful [1].

[2 marks] Explain how a catalyst increases the rate of a reaction.

A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway [1]; with a lower activation energy, so a greater proportion of particles have sufficient energy to react [1].
