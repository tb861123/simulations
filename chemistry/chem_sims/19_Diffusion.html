Key Facts

NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl (white ring)
Particles move randomly, spreading from high → low concentration
Lighter particles (smaller Mr) diffuse faster
NH₃ (Mr 17) < HCl (Mr 36.5) → ring nearer HCl end
Higher temperature → faster diffusion
Exam Practice

[3 marks] Cotton wool soaked in concentrated ammonia and concentrated hydrochloric acid are placed at opposite ends of a glass tube. Explain where the white ring forms and why.

A white ring of ammonium chloride forms [1]; it forms nearer the hydrochloric acid end [1]; because ammonia has a smaller relative molecular mass (is lighter), so it diffuses faster and travels further before they meet [1].

[2 marks] What does the diffusion of gases tell us about the movement of particles?

The particles are constantly moving [1]; they move randomly and spread out from a region of high concentration to low concentration [1].

[2 marks] Explain the effect of increasing the temperature on the rate of diffusion.

The particles gain more kinetic energy and move faster [1]; so they diffuse (spread out) more quickly [1].
