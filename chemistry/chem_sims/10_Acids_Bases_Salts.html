Exam Practice

[2 marks] Write the ionic equation for the neutralisation of any acid by any alkali, and name the type of reaction.

H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l) [1]; neutralisation [1].

[3 marks] Describe how you would use a titration to find the exact volume of alkali needed to neutralise 25 cm³ of acid.

Pipette 25 cm³ of acid into a conical flask and add a few drops of indicator [1]; add alkali from a burette, swirling, until the indicator just changes colour (the end point) [1]; record the volume; repeat until concordant results are obtained [1].

[2 marks] Explain why phenolphthalein is a suitable indicator for a strong acid–strong alkali titration.

Its colour change (around pH 8–10) falls within the steep, near-vertical part of the titration curve [1]; so it changes sharply with a single drop at the end point, giving an accurate result [1].
