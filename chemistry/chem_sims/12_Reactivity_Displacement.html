Key Facts

more reactive metal + salt of less reactive metal → salt of more reactive metal + less reactive metal
A metal displaces any metal below it in the reactivity series
Displacement is a redox reaction (electrons transferred)
Exam Practice

[3 marks] A piece of zinc is placed in copper(II) sulfate solution. Describe what you would observe and write the ionic equation.

The blue solution fades / goes paler and a reddish-brown coating (copper) forms on the zinc [1]; the zinc gradually dissolves and the mixture warms up [1]; Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu [1].

[2 marks] Explain why no reaction occurs when copper is placed in magnesium sulfate solution.

Copper is less reactive than magnesium [1]; so copper cannot displace magnesium from its salt solution [1].

[3 marks] Displacement reactions are redox reactions. Explain what happens to the zinc atoms and the copper ions in terms of electrons.

Zinc atoms lose electrons (oxidised): Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ [1]; copper ions gain electrons (reduced): Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu [1]; electrons are transferred from zinc to copper ions [1].
