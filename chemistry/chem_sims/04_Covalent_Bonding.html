Exam Practice

[2 marks] Draw a dot-and-cross diagram for water (H₂O), showing the outer electrons only.

Oxygen at the centre [1]; each hydrogen shares one pair of electrons with the oxygen, and oxygen has two lone pairs of electrons [1].

[3 marks] Explain why methane (CH₄) has a low boiling point.

Methane is a simple molecular substance [1]; there are weak intermolecular forces between molecules [1]; little energy is needed to overcome these forces [1].

[2 marks] Explain why diamond is very hard.

Each carbon atom is covalently bonded to four others in a tetrahedral arrangement [1]; forming a rigid giant covalent lattice that is very difficult to distort [1].
