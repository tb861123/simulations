Key Equations

ΔH = energy(bonds broken) − energy(bonds made)
Exothermic: releases heat, ΔH negative
Endothermic: takes in heat, ΔH positive
Bonds break = endo (in) · bonds make = exo (out)
Catalyst lowers Eₐ, ΔH unchanged
Exam Practice

[3 marks] Explain, in terms of bond breaking and bond making, why the combustion of methane is exothermic.

Breaking bonds in the reactants is endothermic (takes in energy) [1]; making bonds in the products is exothermic (releases energy) [1]; more energy is released making bonds than is taken in breaking them, so overall energy is released [1].

[2 marks] A student adds a catalyst to a reaction. State the effect on the activation energy and on ΔH.

The activation energy is lowered [1]; ΔH (the overall energy change) is unchanged [1].

[3 marks] Using bond energies, calculate ΔH for a reaction where 1650 kJ of bonds are broken and 1800 kJ of bonds are made. State whether it is exothermic or endothermic.

ΔH = energy in − energy out = 1650 − 1800 [1]; ΔH = −150 kJ/mol [1]; exothermic (ΔH is negative) [1].
