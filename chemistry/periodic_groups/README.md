# Groups 1 and 7

A single, self-contained HTML file for iGCSE chemistry with an A level option.
It needs no install and no internet: **open `periodic-groups.html` in Chrome
or Edge**.

★ Enhanced: built from `tools/kit/src/periodic_groups.js` by `node build.js`
(run inside `tools/kit/`). Do not edit `periodic-groups.html` by hand. The
earlier version and its worksheets are kept in [`original/`](original/).
Metal displacement and the reactivity series are in
[Reactivity and Extracting Metals](../reactivity_metals/).

- **Standard (iGCSE):** Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1)
  2(a) Group 1 and 2(b) Group 7.
- **Advanced (A level):** 9CH0 Topic 4: trends down Groups 1, 2 and 7;
  halogen displacement as redox with half-equations and E°.

## Views

- **💧 Group 1 + water:** lithium to caesium, mass of the piece, volume and
  temperature of the water. The piece floats or sinks, melts into a ball,
  fizzes, burns with its flame colour (K, Rb, Cs) or explodes (Cs); the
  universal indicator turns purple. Chart: hydrogen and temperature against
  time. The atom is drawn with its outer electron.
- **🫙 Halogens: states:** fluorine to astatine at any temperature (−250 to
  350 °C), as solid, liquid or gas, with their colours; chart of melting and
  boiling points.
- **🧪 Halogens: displacement:** the 3 × 3 grid of chlorine, bromine and
  iodine water added to KCl, KBr and KI, with real colours and an optional
  hexane layer; chart of the percentage that reacted.
- **Trend chart** (all views): atomic radius, first ionisation energy, melting
  and boiling points, density or electronegativity down Group 1, 2 or 7.

## The model and its simplifications

Data from tables (francium and astatine estimated). The piece shrinks at a
steady speed (Arrhenius in temperature); hydrogen ½(m/Ar) × 24 000 cm³;
temperature rise n|ΔH| × 0.7 ÷ (m × 4.18); pH = 14 + log₁₀[OH⁻]. Displacement:
equilibrium K = e^(2FΔE°/RT), solved exactly. The earlier version's A level box
(Group 2 chemistry, thermal stability, flame tests, halides with sulfuric
acid, unknown salt) is not included; Group 2 trends are in the trend chart.
