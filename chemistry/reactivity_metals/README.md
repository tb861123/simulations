# Reactivity and Extracting Metals

The reactivity series from start to finish: metals with water, steam and acid
(hydrogen in a gas syringe), displacement reactions in salt solutions (with the
temperature rise and the electrons that move), and how each metal is extracted.

## How to run

Open `reactivity-metals.html` in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install; works offline.

## What it shows

- The series across the top, coloured by extraction method.
- **Water and acid**: eleven metals with cold water, steam or dilute HCl;
  ribbon or powder, mass, acid concentration; bubbles, gas syringe, curves to
  keep and compare, hydrogen in the first minute by metal.
- **Displacement**: magnesium, zinc, iron, copper and silver with their salt
  solutions; colour change, metal deposited, temperature rise, a results table;
  half-equations and oxidising/reducing agents at A level.
- **Extraction**: the blast furnace, electrolysis of aluminium, metals found
  native; when each metal was first isolated; how common each is in the crust.
- Eight guided investigations and a Revise tab.

## Batch run

Reactivity and rate with acid; mass of magnesium against volume of hydrogen
(991 cm³ per g against 988 from the equation); acid concentration and rate;
temperature rise against distance apart in the series (a straight line).

for the enthalpy change. See "The model" tab.
