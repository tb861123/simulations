4 · Tuned mass damper in earthquakes

How much does a tuned mass damper reduce drift in 200 random earthquakes? Each pair of runs uses the same earthquake with and without a 5% damper (Den Hartog tuning). A damper is best at steady resonant shaking; in short, random earthquakes it helps less, because it needs a few cycles to start swinging. Results scatter widely because every earthquake is different; compare the % of runs past the cracking limit.

5 · Base isolation for a hospital

Does base isolation protect a 4-storey hospital in strong quakes? Isolation stretches the period to 2.5 s, well away from the quick shaking on rock, so floor accelerations and drifts fall dramatically. The price is a big movement at the bearings (see Bearing movement). On soft soil with slow shaking isolation helps much less.
