# Pitch, Notes and Intervals

Connect a playable keyboard, staff positions, semitones and frequency ratios.

Open `pitch_intervals.html` in Chrome or Edge. Works offline. Press Play to enable sound.

## Investigation
Compare unison, octave, fifth and semitone. Transpose the root while keeping the interval fixed. Does the frequency difference stay fixed? Does the ratio?

## Model
In twelve-tone equal temperament, f = 440 × 2^((m−69)/12). An octave doubles frequency. Equal semitone steps multiply frequency by the same ratio.

Western twelve-tone equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz. Staff uses treble clef and interval-aware upper-note spelling. The root uses sharp spellings. The tritone is spelled as an augmented fourth. Other enharmonic spellings depend on context.
