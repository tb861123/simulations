# Inside a Sound

Change pitch, level and waveform; watch air particles and inspect the spectrum.

Open `inside_sound.html` in Chrome or Edge. Works offline. Press Play to enable sound.

## Investigation
Compare sine and square waves at the same frequency. Which has more upper partials? Then double the frequency: what happens to the spacing of compressions?

## Model
Frequency sets the repetition rate. Amplitude is not perceived loudness: hearing and playback equipment also matter. Air particles oscillate locally; the disturbance travels.

Waveforms are ideal periodic signals, truncated to 16 partials. Particle motion and time are exaggerated and slowed; sound plays at the actual frequency.
