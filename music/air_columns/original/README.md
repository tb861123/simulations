# Air Columns

Explore open and closed tubes, effective length and standing pressure waves.

Open `air_columns.html` in Chrome or Edge. Works offline. Press Play to enable sound.

## Investigation
Compare the two tubes at the same length. Open a hole halfway down: what happens to the effective length? Step through the resonances and compare their spacing.

## Model
Open ends are pressure nodes. A closed end is a pressure antinode. Ideal open tubes have fₙ = nc/(2L); ideal closed tubes have fₙ = (2n−1)c/(4L).

One-dimensional ideal tube, c = 331 + 0.6T m/s. A hole is treated as a perfect open termination. Real tone holes, bore shape, end corrections, reeds and jet excitation require more complex models. The sound is the selected resonance, not a realistic instrument timbre.
